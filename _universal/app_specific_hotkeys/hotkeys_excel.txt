app-specific hotkeys: excel

========================================================================
S + A TIER
========================================================================
ctrl-shift-+                           ||  Insert new row
ctrl-shift-(+), (for table)        ||  Insert new row in an exisiting table
ctrl-pageup/pagedown                   ||  Iterate through sheets
f2                                     ||  Enter active cell
alt-h-6                                ||  indent cell more (do Alt + H + 5' to 'decrease')
'                                      ||  force cell to be 'text-only' and ignore all macro shit
ctrl-1                                 ||  Open 'Format Cells' menu

ctrl-j (my custom VBA)               ||  pulls up menu of ALL the SHEETS, and can scroll to select which one to jump to

alt then n,p,o,d                       ||  choose img to insert from device (faster than clicking through the 5 diff menus)(Alt + N + P + O + D)

========================================================================
WHILE EDITING INSIDE A CELL...
========================================================================
home                                   ||  Jump cursor to 'beginning' of cell
end                                    ||  Jump cursor to 'end' of cell
alt-enter                              ||  Insert line break within cell
