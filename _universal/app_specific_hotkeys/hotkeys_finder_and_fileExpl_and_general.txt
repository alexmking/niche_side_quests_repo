========================================================================
S + A TIER
========================================================================

ctrl-w	            || Close window
win-shift-m	        || unhide all minimzied windows 
win-,	            || Show Desktop


win-shift-`	                                || launch fancy zone editor
win-ctrl-alt-(number 1-9)	                || set this desktop to fancy zone layout (1 thru 9)
shift-(while dragging a window)	            || (normal use case) see curr fancy zones
shift-(while dragging a window) + 1	        || switch to custom layout 1
shift-(while dragging a window) + 2	        || switch to custom layout 2
shift-(while dragging a window) + 3	        || switch to custom layout 3
shift-(while dragging a window) + 4 ... 9	|| switch to custom layout … 4 through 9
